The psychology of healthy, engaged and meaningful work

I am an Associate Professor at Nyenrode Business University and affiliated with the Knowledge Institute of Work and Health at Arbo Unie, the Netherlands. I received my PhD in psychology from Utrecht University on motivation at work.

Research

Using large-scale occupational health data, I study which job characteristics foster work engagement and protect against burnout, within the Job Demands-Resources framework. Current projects examine how hybrid work shifts the weight of meaningful work for engagement and turnover intention, and which factors predict sickness absence due to mental health complaints. Underlying this work is a long-standing interest in leadership, basic psychological needs, values and organizational purpose.
