About

I am affiliated with the Knowledge Institute of Work and Health of Arbo Unie and an Associate Professor at Nyenrode Business University, the Netherlands. I study the psychology of leadership, motivation, meaning and sustainable engagement at work. What drives me: showing how small changes in self-awareness, behaviour and the work environment can strengthen motivation and meaning.

At Arbo Unie I use occupational health data from tens of thousands of workers to study how working conditions, engagement and health are related. At Nyenrode I challenge students and executives to reflect on their values and impact, and to translate those insights into their leadership.

I received my PhD in psychology from Utrecht University on work motivation. My dissertation shows how engaging leadership and a clear corporate purpose strengthen employee engagement through basic psychological needs and intrinsic values.

My path to academia ran through practice. I graduated cum laude in political science (political theory) from the University of Amsterdam. I then worked in the semi-public sector and in telecommunications, most recently as business development manager. There I was responsible, among other things, for international internet strategy and led mergers and acquisitions in internet and media. While still a student, I also worked as a chef in fine restaurants, where I learned what real teamwork under pressure means.

Coaching then became my profession. As an executive coach I worked with leaders and teams in industries including automotive, financial services, oil and gas, telecom, pharma and retail. I was a mentor and program coach with Newfield Network, a leading American-Chilean coaching school, working in their programs in Asia, Europe, South Africa and the United States. At International Christian University in Tokyo I was a coach and guest lecturer in the Global Leadership Program, and at the Center for Creative Leadership an executive coach and leadership associate. I completed postgraduate programs at Ashridge and IMD Lausanne and am certified as a Professional Certified Coach (ICF) and Newfield Certified Ontological Coach.

All those years in practice gave me the question that still drives my research: why do people flourish in one job and burn out in another?
