About

“Meaningful work is where skill meets purpose.”

I am affiliated with the Knowledge Institute of Work and Health of Arbo Unie and an Associate Professor at Nyenrode Business University, the Netherlands. I study the psychology of leadership, motivation, meaning and sustainable engagement at work. What drives me: showing how small changes in self-awareness, behaviour and the work environment can strengthen motivation and meaning.

At Arbo Unie I use occupational health data from tens of thousands of workers to study how working conditions, engagement and health are related. At Nyenrode I challenge students and executives to reflect on their values and impact, and to translate those insights into their leadership.

I received my PhD in psychology from Utrecht University on work motivation. My dissertation shows how engaging leadership and a clear corporate purpose strengthen employee engagement through basic psychological needs and intrinsic values.

My path to academia ran through practice. After studying political science I worked in telecommunications and the internet sector. I then spent many years as an executive coach (ICF), among others for Newfield Network, in the Global Leadership Program of International Christian University in Tokyo, and for the Center for Creative Leadership.
